About MyShopGuard

MyShopGuard was engineered to solve a common daily challenge faced by local shop owners and micro-retailers: managing credit sales and tracking customer debts without the frustration of lost paper notebooks or calculation errors.

Built with a deep focus on user experience and data privacy, our platform guarantees that vendors can seamlessly record transactions, instantly calculate balances, and follow up on payments securely using PIN protection.

Designed as a modern digital solution for community commerce, MyShopGuard bridges everyday trade with reliable web technology.
